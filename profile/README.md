# Open AI Directory

### Curated directories of the best AI tools for real-world work

**Open AI Directory** is an independent, community-maintained collection of curated AI tool directories organized by profession and industry.

We research, categorize, and maintain practical lists of **AI tools, AI software, AI agents, automation platforms, and generative AI products** for developers, healthcare teams, legal professionals, marketers, finance teams, educators, HR teams, and designers.

Our goal is simple:

> **Make it easier to discover useful AI tools without digging through hundreds of low-quality listings, abandoned products, or paid placements.**

Each directory focuses on a specific professional use case and is designed to help people discover, compare, and evaluate AI products relevant to their work.

## Why Open AI Directory?

The AI ecosystem is growing quickly, but discovering the right software is becoming harder.

Search results are often dominated by generic listicles, affiliate pages, outdated recommendations, and enormous databases where every product is treated equally.

Open AI Directory takes a different approach.

### Curated by use case

Each repository focuses on a specific profession or industry rather than trying to place every AI product into one enormous database.

### Practical descriptions

Listings explain what a product actually does and the workflow it is designed to improve.

### Community maintained

Corrections, new tools, improvements, and category suggestions can be submitted through GitHub.

### Regularly reviewed

Directories are reviewed periodically so abandoned products, broken links, and outdated information can be corrected or removed.

### No pay-to-list rankings

Payment does not determine editorial ranking.

Where sponsorships exist, sponsored placements should be clearly identified and kept separate from editorial judgment.

## Editorial Principles

A useful directory requires more than collecting links.

We prioritize tools that meet practical criteria such as:

**1. The product exists**

We prefer live products that people can actually evaluate or use—not placeholder websites or permanently unreleased projects.

**2. The tool fits the category**

A general-purpose chatbot does not automatically belong in every industry directory. Products should have a meaningful use case for the audience of that repository.

**3. Information should be useful**

Listings should describe the product clearly enough for someone to understand why they might choose it.

**4. Commercial relationships should be transparent**

Sponsored placements should be labeled and should not silently influence editorial recommendations.

**5. The community should be able to correct us**

GitHub issues and pull requests make the directories open to correction, verification, and improvement.

## For AI Tool Companies

Built an AI product that belongs in one of our directories?

We welcome relevant submissions.

Before submitting, choose the directory that most closely matches the product's **primary real-world use case** and review that repository's contribution guidelines.

### Submit an AI Tool

1. Open the relevant directory.
2. Read its contribution requirements.
3. Check that your product is not already listed.
4. Submit the requested information.
5. Open a pull request or use the repository's submission process.

Accurate, specific submissions are much more useful than promotional copy.

Explain **what the product does, who it is for, what problem it solves, and how it differs from alternatives**.
